# Review methodology — verification status

**Review type:** systematic review of empirical Agentic MLOps research. **Target journal:** ACM Computing Surveys. **Frozen original working corpus:** 559 records.

## Eligibility operationalization
A candidate investigation should report:
1. An ML lifecycle action, e.g. reproducible offline experimentation, deployment, monitoring, remediation or pipeline operation.
2. Autonomous operational decision selection that changes with feedback/history, rather than deterministic threshold-only automation.
3. An implemented empirical system and evaluation appropriate to the review's original eligibility protocol.
4. A suitable executed comparator where the agreed protocol requires one. Missing comparator or agent-specific ablation may constrain comparative/causal claims, but should not be silently redefined into a retrospective exclusion rule.

LLM usage and multi-agent architecture are **neither necessary nor sufficient** for agentic eligibility. ML-model adaptation alone is not evidence of adaptive operational decision-making.

## Protocol and amendments
The precise original WoS executable query, run date, database indexes, filters, duplicate-removal history, original eligibility protocol version and amendments have **not been independently recovered**. Do not describe later clarifications as preregistered or prespecified without protocol evidence.

## Selection and evidence stages
- Record-level title/abstract screening and decision provenance;
- report retrieval and report-level eligibility, with reasons for full-text exclusion;
- mapping multiple reports to unique studies;
- independent reviewer reconciliation; inter-rater statistics only from nonblank paired votes;
- study-outcome-comparator extraction with page/section anchors;
- seven-domain methodological appraisal;
- AMEAF claim-level interpretation; no compensation across required evidence dimensions.

## Seven methodological appraisal domains
Construct validity; data integrity; comparator fairness; causal attribution; statistical precision; external validity; reproducibility. Missing information is **unclear/not assessable**, not a demonstrated methodological failure.

## Evidence boundaries
- Historical 532 decisions, including 516 exclusion labels, 10 full-text inclusion labels and 6 provisional labels, plus 27 unresolved, are historical tracking categories only.
- The original 559-row working master does not by itself verify 559 unique study reports or the actual search-return count before deduplication.
- Comparative illustrations: four candidate investigations and 15 contrasts, not an independent 15-study sample or an effect-size meta-analysis.
- Source-level formal risk-of-bias ratings, independent agreement and final included-study denominator are pending.

**No empirical findings, reviewer votes, uncertainty intervals or eligibility decisions are synthesized by this documentation.**
